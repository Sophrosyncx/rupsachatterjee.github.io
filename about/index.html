About me

Curiosity at the boundaries.

I am Rupsa Chatterjee, a fourth year Integrated Masters student at IISER Pune. I am interested in learning and, hopefully, researching in Physics, along with doing science education research.

My work moves between science and science education, with a particular interest in physics, computation, modelling, and the ways people learn to reason about complex ideas. I enjoy taking ideas that are often presented as abstract or inaccessible and asking whether they can be made more tangible through models, simulations, visualisations, or carefully constructed explanations.

I am also interested in the space between research and making. A model is not only something that represents an idea; it can also become a way of thinking with that idea. This is why I build computational models and simulations alongside more conventional forms of research and writing.

This website is a collection of that work: my research, published writing, talks, computational projects, notes, and resources. It is also a record of questions I am currently exploring—some with answers, many still without them.
